BOTTOM LINE

Our product is stronger than Canvas or Blackboard — a true all-in-one platform they can't match. But our website still sells to India (en_IN, INR, NAAC/TnP), with zero FERPA, zero U.S. proof, and no pricing in dollars. A U.S. buyer dismisses us in 5 seconds. This is a marketing fix, not a product rebuild — solvable in 60–90 days.

What We're Lacking (the 5 that cost us deals)

!

U.S. localization — en_IN locale, INR pricing, and India-only terms (TnP, NAAC, College ERP) signal "not built for you."

!

FERPA & security — absent entirely. It's the first thing every U.S. institution checks; its absence ends the conversation.

!

U.S. proof — no American logos, testimonials, or G2/Capterra badges. Canvas claims "50% of NA students + every Ivy."

!

Content & conversion — no pricing, about, blog, or clear "Request a Demo" path. Both rivals run deep libraries.

!

Emotional story — we lead with features; they sell educator outcomes. We read transactional by comparison.

Where We Win

✓

The all-in-one story — Canvas is LMS + bolt-ons; Blackboard is T&L only. We're genuinely unified (LMS+SIS+CRM+AI). No one else can claim it.

✓

Trust transparency — both rivals gate or bury their compliance. A clear public FERPA/security page out-positions them on what buyers check first.

✓

Incumbent instability — Blackboard just exited Chapter 11 bankruptcy; Banner/Blackboard fatigue is high. We are the fresh, affordable, modern choice — perfect for community colleges.

Recommended 90-Day Brand Sprint

1
Days 1–30 · Foundation
Go U.S.-native: en_US + USD, strip India terms, publish a FERPA/security/accessibility trust page, add a "Request a Demo" CTA, and lead the hero with the all-in-one story.
2
Days 30–60 · Proof
Build credibility: list on G2/Capterra, land 1–2 reference institutions for logos/testimonials, and add pricing/about/blog pages targeting "Banner alternative" and "Blackboard alternative" searches.
3
Days 60–90 · Authority
Compound it: start SOC 2 Type II, pursue EDUCAUSE listing, stand up a content engine, and develop one bold, defensible proof stat as pilots convert.
The Ask

Approve the 90-day U.S. brand sprint and name an owner. No product investment required — this is marketing, content, and compliance display. Keep the current gold/navy identity (it scores well); the work is localization, proof, and depth. Target: move from 3/10 to a competitive U.S. brand presence by end of Q3, in time to convert the community-college beachhead.
