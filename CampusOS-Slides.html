FERPA compliance is non-negotiable — and currently missing
Every North American institution will ask about FERPA before anything else. It's the first filter in every procurement conversation.

⚠️  FERPA, SOC 2, ADA/WCAG, and US data residency are not mentioned anywhere on campusos.co. A buyer who searches for FERPA compliance and finds nothing will move to the next vendor immediately.

🔒
FERPA Compliance
Federal student data privacy law · Must be front and center on the homepage and pricing page · CRITICAL — fix first
✅
SOC 2 Type II
Required by most IT procurement teams before any contract is signed · Begin audit process immediately
♿
ADA / WCAG 2.1 Accessibility
Legal requirement for all federally funded institutions · Non-compliance creates legal liability for buyers
🏛️
US Data Residency
Many state institutions cannot use platforms without US-hosted data · Must be explicitly addressed

Community colleges: underserved, motivated, and ready to buy
The beachhead that makes the rest of the market accessible
1
Single decision-maker — faster sales
Unlike large universities with 12-person procurement committees, community college IT decisions are often made by one or two people. Sales cycles are dramatically shorter.
2
Acutely underserved by Banner
Most community colleges are running outdated Banner implementations with support costs that consume a disproportionate share of their IT budget. They are actively looking for alternatives.
3
Strong word-of-mouth networks
The community college world is tight-knit. AACC and League for Innovation conferences are where decisions get made. A few happy customers become a pipeline.
4
CampusOS pricing is a perfect fit
Budget-conscious institutions respond strongly to the value proposition of an all-in-one platform at a fraction of incumbent costs.
