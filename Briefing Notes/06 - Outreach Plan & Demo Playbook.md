## Pre-Flight Hard Gate

⚠️ **Do not send a single email until all three are live:**

- [ ] FERPA compliance page live at `campusos.co/compliance` — linked from homepage header
- [ ] USD pricing visible — zero INR showing anywhere
- [ ] Calendly booking link set up and tested end-to-end

## 4 Audience Segments

| Segment | Who | Hook | Priority |
|---------|-----|------|----------|
| **A** | Community Colleges | "Replace Banner in 30 days — not 18 months" | 🔴 1st |
| **B** | Blackboard Users | "With Blackboard's bankruptcy — what's your plan?" | 🔴 1st |
| **C** | Small 4-Year Private | "Campus software finally built for your size" | 🟡 2nd |
| **D** | General / Unknown LMS | "How many tools is your campus running right now?" | 🟢 3rd |

**Start with Segments B and A simultaneously.** B has the most urgency (Blackboard anxiety). A has the largest total addressable market.

---

## 5-Touch Sequence — 21 Days

| Day | Channel | What |
|-----|---------|------|
| **Day 1** | Email | Segment-specific pain hook. No meeting ask yet. |
| **Day 3** | LinkedIn | 1-sentence connection note. No pitch. No link. |
| **Day 7** | Email | Free FERPA checklist drop. "No reply needed." |
| **Day 14** | Email | First direct demo ask + Calendly link. |
| **Day 21** | Email | 3-sentence breakup. Creates finality — triggers reply. |

> The breakup email (Day 21) consistently generates more replies than any other touch. Keep it exactly 3 sentences. No pitch. Just a clean close.

---

## 30-Minute Demo Script

| Time | Step | Goal |
|------|------|------|
| 0–5 min | Ask 2 discovery questions about their current frustrations | Understand their pain *before* showing anything |
| 5–15 min | Live demo — one login: LMS → SIS → AI alert → Career Services | "Notice you never switch tools" |
| 15–22 min | Cost story — ROI calculator vs. Banner / Blackboard | Let the numbers close the emotional case |
| 22–27 min | FERPA + Trust — pull up `campusos.co/compliance` live | "Publicly documented — no login required unlike Canvas" |
| 27–30 min | Clear next step — pilot offer, proposal, or IT call | **Never end without a named next action and a date** |

---

## The Two Proof Assets to Lead Every Demo With

1. **UMF** — "Here's a real US university running on CampusOS today"
2. **iNova** — "Here's our own academy, live on the platform — you can log in right now"

These two statements eliminate the two most common early objections:
- *"Do you have US references?"* → UMF
- *"Is this platform actually proven?"* → iNova live on screen
