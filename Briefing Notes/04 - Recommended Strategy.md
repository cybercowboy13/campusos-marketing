## Three Tracks Evaluated

| Track | Approach | Verdict |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **A — Market Now** | Begin outreach immediately while fixing site in parallel | ⛔ Not Recommended |
| **B — Wait & Fix** | Fix site fully, then begin outreach in 2–3 weeks | ✅ Acceptable |
| **C — Bridge Strategy** | Fix site + build iNova programs simultaneously; outreach when both ready | ✅✅ **Recommended** |

**Track A** is tempting because Blackboard contacts are warm — but sending outreach before the site is US-ready burns those leads for 12–24 months.

---

## Track C — The Bridge Strategy

Two parallel tracks running simultaneously, outreach launching when both are ready:

```
Track 1 (Site)    → Fix locale, pricing, FERPA, terminology → GO
Track 2 (iNova)   → Build 3 bridge programs on CampusOS    → LIVE DEMO
                                ↓
                         Launch Outreach
                    "Book 20 demos in 60 days"
```

---

## Why the Bridge Strategy Wins

### UMF as the Portfolio Anchor

- Real US institution — live on CampusOS today
- Eliminates the "no US references" objection in every demo
- A UMF case study is the most credible content we can publish
- **One US university logo on the homepage transforms brand perception overnight**

### iNova Programs as Live Proof

- Running our own academy on the platform = living demo, not screenshots
- Generates B2C tuition revenue while the B2B pipeline builds
- Forces real-world platform testing before a major college client hits an issue
- **"We built our academy on it" is the strongest possible proof statement**

---

## The Two-Asset Demo

Every college demo should show two things:

1. **UMF** — "Here's a real US university running on CampusOS today"
2. **iNova** — "Here's our own academy, live on the platform — you can log in and look around"

No slide deck accomplishes what a live walkthrough of a running institution does.

## Revenue Model (Three Channels)

| Channel | Revenue Source | Timeline | Strategic Value |
|---------|---------------|----------|----------------|
| **B2C — iNova** | Individual student tuition | Month 1–2 | Immediate revenue + platform proof |
| **B2B — CampusOS** | College licensing (LMS + SIS + Career Services) | Month 2–6 | Primary long-term revenue engine |
| **Cross-Sell** | iNova programs licensed as workforce training to CampusOS clients | Month 6+ | Recurring upsell + retention driver |
