## Competitive Brand Scorecard

### Canvas / Instructure — 9/10
- 50%+ of US college students, every Ivy League
- Dedicated Trust Portal with full compliance docs
- $900M+ annual revenue
- **Weakness:** Compliance documentation gated behind a login; enterprise pricing excludes small schools

### Blackboard / Anthology — 7/10
- Large legacy install base
- ⚠️ Emerged from Chapter 11 bankruptcy **March 2026** — third rebrand in a few years
- Institutions are quietly evaluating alternatives right now
- **Weakness:** Stability uncertainty, pricing, legacy architecture

### CampusOS — 3/10 (brand readiness, not product quality)
- Strong product — every gap identified is fixable
- Score can reach **7/10 within 90 days** with focused execution
- One US university reference (UMF) already in hand

> The 3/10 is a brand readiness score, not a product score. The path to 7/10 is clear and timed.
