## The Stackable Credential Model

Each program runs in two tiers:
- **Short Certificate** — 4–6 weeks, fast-to-market, high-demand, immediate enrollment
- **Full Credential** — 5–6 months, deeper outcomes, stacks from the short cert

Students enter at either tier. This mirrors the format US workforce development programs favor — and positions iNova for partnerships with the same community colleges we're approaching for CampusOS licensing.

---

## Program 1 — Business Management + AI Leadership

> **Audience:** Emerging leaders navigating AI-transformed workplaces  
> **Priority:** Build first — broadest audience, fastest to build

### Short Certificate (4–6 Weeks)
1. Business Fundamentals in the AI Era
2. Leading AI-Integrated Teams
3. Data-Driven Decision Making
4. AI Tools for Business Productivity
5. Capstone: AI Business Strategy Project

### Full Credential (5–6 Months) — adds:
- Strategic Planning with AI
- Change Management & Digital Transformation
- Financial Analysis with AI Tools
- AI Ethics, Risk & Governance
- Operations Management in the AI Age
- Final Project: AI Integration Business Plan

## Why Three Programs, Not One

- **Diversifies the demo** — different programs appeal to different college departments
- **Covers the workforce development trifecta:** business, security, and technical tracks
- **Cross-sell opportunity:** once CampusOS colleges sign, they can license iNova programs as workforce training for their own students
- Each running program is another piece of live proof in every demo
