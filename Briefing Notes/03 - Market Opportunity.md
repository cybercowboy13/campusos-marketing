## There Has Never Been a Better Window

Four simultaneous conditions have opened a lane that didn't exist 18 months ago.

---

### 1. Blackboard's Bankruptcy Window 🔴

- Blackboard / Anthology emerged from **Chapter 11 bankruptcy in March 2026** — their **third rebrand** in a few years
- Institutions on active contracts are planning their next move 6–12 months out
- The question every IT department is quietly asking right now: *"What happens to our data and support if Blackboard struggles again?"*
- This is an open door for a stable, modern alternative
- **This window is real but not infinite — it's still open in 3 weeks**

---

### 2. Canvas Compliance Gating 🔵

- Canvas requires a **login to access compliance and security documentation**
- CampusOS can own the position of **"most transparent compliant vendor"** by publishing everything publicly — no login required
- FERPA status, SOC 2 roadmap, ADA/WCAG statement — all public-facing
- This directly attacks Canvas's weakest perception point with procurement teams

---

### 3. Community College Price Gap 🟡

- **Banner SIS:** $500K–$5M per year at most community colleges
- **Canvas** is an additional cost on top
- **CampusOS replaces both** — LMS + SIS + Career Services in one license — at a fraction of the cost
- This is not a marginal pricing advantage — it's a **structural one**
- CFOs and budget-constrained provosts respond to this immediately

---

### 4. AI-Native Positioning 🟢

- Canvas and Blackboard have **bolt-on AI** — features layered onto legacy architectures
- CampusOS has **AI student success tools built in from the ground up** — one platform, one login
- Community colleges face enrollment pressure and student retention challenges
- AI-driven early alerts and outcome tracking are moving from "nice to have" to **procurement priority**

---

## The Open Lane

> Community colleges and small universities (under 10,000 students) are **underserved by both Canvas and Blackboard**. Canvas is priced for large research universities. Blackboard is in stability uncertainty. There is no dominant modern, affordable, FERPA-compliant, AI-integrated platform serving small institutions.
>
> **That lane is open. CampusOS can own it.**

---

## Target Market Size

- ~1,000 community colleges in the US
- Thousands of small 4-year private institutions
- Most are running Banner ($500K–$5M/yr) + Canvas or Blackboard
- Every one of them is a replacement opportunity
